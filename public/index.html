How It Works

From Waste to Valuable Resource

Our machinery supports each stage of turning suitable waste materials into recovered oil, protein-rich meal and other usable products.

01

Waste Intake & Size Reduction

Suitable poultry, hatchery, eggshell or food waste is prepared through size-reduction stages.

02

Cooking & Rendering

Rendering systems process suitable animal-origin by-products into recovered fats and protein-rich materials, depending on the raw material and selected process.

03

Drying

Industrial drying reduces moisture content using the appropriate heating and drying technology.

04

Oil Extraction & Recovery

Oil recovery and extraction machinery separates recoverable fats from suitable processed materials.

Process-integrated odour collection and treatment supports improved working conditions throughout.
